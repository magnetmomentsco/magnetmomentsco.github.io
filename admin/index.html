🎯 Visitor Intent

▼
ℹ How scoring works: Each visitor accumulates intent points as they interact with the site. Points are persisted across pages via localStorage.
High (10+ pts) — Likely buyer. Viewed multiple products, scrolled deep, spent 60s+ on site, or added to cart.
Medium (5–9 pts) — Interested browser. Visited a few pages, hovered on products, or browsed images.
Low (<5 pts) — Casual visitor. Viewed 1–2 pages with minimal engagement.
Points: +1 per page view · +2 for deep scroll (75%+) · +2 for product hover · +3 for product view · +2 for image browse · +5 for add-to-cart · +2 after 60s on site
